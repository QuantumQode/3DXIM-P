# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Researchers, engineers, and industry partners evaluating whether to collaborate with or commission work from the lab — academics in materials science, geology, cultural heritage, and biomedical fields; PhD students and postdocs researching a placement; industry engineers needing non-destructive characterization of components. Inferred from site content (facility specs, publication list, team credentials); not confirmed via interview.

## Product Purpose
3DXIM is the X-ray imaging laboratory of ZAG (Slovenian National Building and Civil Engineering Institute). The site exists to demonstrate the lab's imaging capabilities and scientific output so visitors can judge fit for a collaboration, scan request, or research placement.

## Positioning
Access to two specific, named CT systems (EasyTOM XL 160/230 Ultra; microXCT400) spanning sub-micron to large-component scale, with in-situ/4D and phase-contrast capabilities most single-scale academic CT labs cannot offer, backed by a visible multi-year, multi-domain publication record (concrete, batteries, archaeology, paleontology, bone, ceramics).

## Operating Context
Static informational site, no accounts or transactions. Visitors browse Facilities (equipment specs), Team, Publications, and Research, then contact the lab directly by email.

## Capabilities and Constraints
No CMS or build tooling — plain HTML/CSS/JS per page, now sharing `styles.css`. `research.html` is a placeholder; several team bios and the microXCT400 photos are still placeholder content pending real material from the client.

## Brand Commitments
Name "3DXIM" and its tagline "Seeing materials from the inside" are established and carried forward. No existing logo, palette, or type system to preserve — prior implementation was generic default styling, not a deliberate brand.

## Evidence on Hand
Real photos: hero CT cross-section image (ZAG-mikroXCT.jpg), EasyTOM system photos (EasyTom_1/2/3.jpeg), team headshots. Real publication list with working DOIs (2021–2026). microXCT400 equipment photos and several team bios are explicitly marked placeholder and must stay so until the client supplies real content.

## Product Principles
- Specificity over generic tech polish: real specs, real equipment, real publications carry the site — never invent capabilities, customers, or numbers.
- The visual identity should read as instrument-grade and scientific, not startup-generic, because that's what distinguishes this lab's actual offer.
- Placeholder content stays visibly placeholder rather than being dressed up as finished.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No specific requirement established. Standard WCAG AA contrast and keyboard/focus support applies as baseline practice.
